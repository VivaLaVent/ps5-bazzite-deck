## What's new
> **TEST BUILD.** For TVs that lose their 4K and 120 Hz modes shortly after boot; everyone else stays on the release marked **Latest**.

- **Fix attempt: TVs that drop to HDMI 1.4 modes** a few seconds after boot (only 1080p60 and 1440p60 left, no 4K, no 120 Hz, no VRR; seen on a Beyond TV). The console now tells HDMI 2.x TVs its link settings at every mode change, as a PC does, not only at 4K and 120 Hz.

## Testing
After a cold start, are 4K and 120 Hz modes offered in Game Mode → Settings → Display? Post the log from [docs/display-logs.md](https://wiki.ps5-linux.eu/troubleshooting/display-recovery#reporting-a-display-problem) either way, with your TV model.
