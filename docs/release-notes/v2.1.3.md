## What's new
- **Bluetooth with a USB Bluetooth dongle plugged in:** the Linux-only address now goes to the console's own adapter. Before, a dongle could take its place in the system's adapter list, and the console's adapter kept the PS5 system's address, so a DualSense's PS5 and Linux pairings collided.

## What to test
With a USB Bluetooth dongle plugged in: `ps5-report | head -12` says `linux-only BT address: on, applied`, and the DualSense keeps its pairings when you switch between the PS5 system and Linux.

## Sharing logs
`ps5-report`, plus `journalctl -u ps5-bt-addr -b --no-pager | tail -5`.
