## What's new
> **Test build for issue #36** (Slim Bluetooth after a warm restart), quick kernel on top of v2.1.3. Not for general use.

- `/sys/kernel/debug/ps5_icc/device_power`: lists the power state of devices 0–31 that the console's system controller manages (only reads), and lets you switch one. Goal: find the device number of the Wi-Fi/Bluetooth module, so Linux can power-cycle it after a warm restart.
