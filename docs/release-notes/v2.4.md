## What's new since v2.1.1

### The bootc image: Bazzite's own way of updating, now on the PS5

v2.4 is the first release with a full **bootc image**: the same PS5 Bazzite Deck, built the way Bazzite itself is built. Instead of an update script replacing files one by one, `sudo bootc upgrade` downloads a complete new system image in the background and switches to it at the next start. Your games, settings and home folder stay as they are. If an update goes wrong, `sudo bootc rollback` puts the previous image back with one command. The PS5 kernel and every PS5-specific fix are part of the image, so they always arrive together and in step.

It comes with the **bazzite-bootc-loader**, built from source for every release: rmux's ps5-linux-loader plus patches for the boot entries bootc writes. It starts existing installs exactly as the standard loader does, so one loader works for both.

The bootc image is experimental. Existing installs keep updating the usual way; trying bootc means flashing a drive (see **Trying the bootc image** below).

### A newer kernel

Linux 7.3.1 with rmux's current PS5 patches, the base most PS5 Linux images build on, and our own work on top:
- **Bluetooth:** newer fixes for the IW620 (original PS5) and MediaTek (Slim, Pro) chips, including one for the Bluetooth timeouts some of you saw
- **4K at 120 Hz** over HDMI 2.1 with automatic fallback when a TV or cable can't keep up, and ALLM (the TV's game mode switches on by itself)
- **RGB range** follows the display's standard "Broadcast RGB" setting
- **PS5 Pro:** CPU and GPU clock drivers with Pro limits
- **Still ours:** VRR on FLAVA3 consoles, the picture coming back after the TV wakes or switches input, keeping the display's details when it briefly reports garbage, the fixes for TVs that fall back to HDMI 1.4, the GPU temperature limit, overlay GPU load and temperature, suspend-to-idle

### Fixes

- **PS5 Pro: D3D9 games work.** Games like Fable Anniversary and NFS Underground hung at the first 3D frame.
- **PS5 Pro: 1 GB VRAM and an 80 °C GPU limit by default,** set once; your own changes stay. Fixes video memory errors in demanding games and power cuts before Linux throttles.
- **The fan no longer spikes to full speed** at shutdown and reboot.
- **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth scripts are as in v2.1.1.** The changes made to them in v2.1.2 to v2.3 caused problems on original PS5s and are withdrawn, along with those releases.
- **No 100 Hz test modes** any more; they caused picture artifacts on Verde consoles.
- **Safer updates:** the update refuses to run on a bootc install, and on an M.2 install it refuses a boot drive that belongs to another install.
- `ps5-report` recognizes every PS5 Pro.

### Changed defaults

Your display's own preferred mode is used instead of 1080p60. `hdmi.rgb_range` is gone; use the display's RGB setting.

## Trying the bootc image

Trying it means reflashing a drive of 64 GB or more (the system alone is about 25 GB).

1. Extract `ps5-bazzite-deck-bootc-v2.4.img.7z.001` with 7-Zip.
2. Flash the `.img`:
   - **Windows:** [Rufus](https://rufus.ie) in **DD mode**, or [Balena Etcher](https://etcher.balena.io)
   - **Linux / macOS:** `sudo dd if=ps5-bazzite-deck-bootc-v2.4.img of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress` (replace `/dev/sdX` with your drive)
3. Start it with `bazzite-bootc-loader-v2.4.elf`.

**First start:** Steam downloads and updates itself before Game Mode appears. That can take 10 to 15 minutes, during which the screen stays black or shows the end of the boot text. Leave it running.

From then on: `sudo bootc upgrade`, then restart. Back to the previous image: `sudo bootc rollback`, then restart. This image follows `:v2.4`; once a release is promoted to stable, `sudo bootc switch ghcr.io/vivalavent/ps5-bazzite-bootc:stable` puts it on regular updates.

Report bootc results in `#bazzite-bootc-testing` on Discord.

## What to test

- **bootc:** flash, start, Game Mode loads; `sudo bootc status` shows the booted image.
- **Original PS5 (IW620):** Wi-Fi and Bluetooth after a cold start, pairing a DualSense, and no `0x2039 tx timeout` lines in `sudo dmesg`.
- **FLAVA3 consoles with an HDMI VRR TV:** VRR in a game with Steam's VRR setting on; 4K at 120 Hz; the picture after the TV wakes or switches input.
- **Verde consoles:** picture at your usual resolution, after sleep and after switching between Game Mode and the desktop.
- **PS5 Pro:** a D3D9 game past its first 3D scene, GPU clock and temperature under load (`ps5-report`), no `mp1fw_sendmsg: Error : busy` flood in `sudo dmesg`.

## Sharing logs

`ps5-report`. For Bluetooth `sudo dmesg | grep -i "hci0\|0x2039"`, for the display `sudo dmesg | grep -iE "hdmi|vrr|frl|edid" | tail -40`, for bootc `sudo bootc status`.
