## What's new
- **Restarts without warm restart enabled go through plain systemd again** (the warm-restart path was involved in every restart before).
- **Bluetooth housekeeping:** one way to keep the adapter connectable instead of two; the hung-adapter fix only runs on original PS5s (IW620), where it's needed.
- **`ps5-report` shows the Linux-only Bluetooth address correctly** (it said "off/n.a." on every console), and `ps5-perf status` explains the PS5 Pro's CPU clock.
- **README for PS5 Pro owners:** start twice after every update; what to do when the loader ends at the PS5 login screen; memory-hungry games.

## What to test
`sudo reboot`: the console restarts as before (with warm restart off it goes through the PS5 system). Bluetooth: the controller reconnects after a restart. `ps5-report | head -12`: the Bluetooth address line says `on, applied` (or `off` if you switched it off).
