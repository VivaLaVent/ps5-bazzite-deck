## What's new
> The new stable release, replacing v1.8.4. Everything below was tested in the v2.0.0 and v2.0.1 pre-releases.

- **One DualSense for the PS5 system and Linux, on by default:** your controller keeps one pairing for each (pair it with Linux once, on another profile, e.g. PS + Square). Fixed: it needed pairing again after cold boots on some consoles. On IW620 consoles, restarts then go through the PS5 system. `ps5-bt-address off` switches it off.
- **PS5 Pro:** the GPU's own firmware now loads (60 compute units; before, most Pros ran as a base PS5 with 36), Bluetooth works from a cold boot, the Linux-only Bluetooth address, the fan driver, GPU clock control with a thermal limit, GPU load in overlays. Needs a loader that dumps the Pro's GPU firmware (see the README).
- **Fan:** a new driver with the console's own fan control as the default (target 80 °C, as before) and an optional fan curve for tools like hwcon and CoolerControl. `ps5-perf status` shows the mode and fan speed.
- **GPU clock:** a new GPU clock governor on every model, with a thermal limit.
- **Display:** 4K120 only where the TV can take it, with automatic fallback to 4K; VRR only on displays with HDMI VRR (as on the PS5 system), and back on TVs that announce no VRR maximum; 100 Hz modes; HDMI link settings at every mode change.
- **CPU boost off is really off** (3.2 GHz).
- **Smaller:** better IW620 keyboard and controller reconnects, Logitech wheel force feedback, no failed services at boot, the updater removes old backups, `ps5-report` with a PS5 Pro section.

## Known issues
- **PS5 Pro:** the 40-CU unlock isn't supported; needs a loader that dumps the GPU firmware.
- **Game Mode's GPU fault when switching sessions at 4K120** on some TVs (a compositor-side bug; keep the desktop at 4K60).
- **VRR on FreeSync-only monitors** and **VRR/4K120 on VERDE consoles** aren't supported.
