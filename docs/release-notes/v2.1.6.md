## What's new
- **Fan no longer briefly spikes to full speed before shutdown or reboot.** The fan driver was re-uploading its original parameter table during cleanup, which caused a brief ramp-up just before the firmware took back control.

## What to test
Shut down and reboot: the fan should stay at its normal speed right until power is cut.

## Sharing logs
If the spike still happens: `sudo dmesg | grep -i "ps5.*fan\|fan.*mode\|wire_mode" | tail -10`.
