## What's new
- **Fix: Bluetooth on original PS5s didn't find new devices** (headsets, keyboards, a second controller), while an already paired DualSense still worked. A Bluetooth change from v2.0 is taken out again.
- **Fix: the fan target reset to 50 °C after sleep**, so the fan got loud after waking the console. The target you had (80 °C by default, or your own) stays now.

## What to test
**Bluetooth (original PS5):** Bluetooth settings → search: devices in pairing mode show up, and a new one pairs.
`ps5-perf status`, then put the console to sleep and wake it, then `ps5-perf status` again: the fan target is the same both times.
