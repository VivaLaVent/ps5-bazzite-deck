## What's new

### Our display code on the new kernel

v2.4 moved to a newer kernel base, and with it to a reworked HDMI handling that stayed black at 4K on some TVs and lost 1440p. v2.5 keeps the newer base but puts back our own display code, the one v2.1.x ran: the same 1080p, 1440p and 4K modes at 60 and 120 Hz, 4K120 over HDMI 2.1, VRR on FLAVA3 consoles, the picture coming back after the TV wakes, and the fixes for TVs that fall back to HDMI 1.4. Everything else stays on the newer base: Bluetooth fixes, PS5 Pro support, fan and clock control.

If you added `hdmi.frl_4k120=0`, `amdgpu.force_1080p=1` or `amdgpu.gpu_recovery=1` to `cmdline.txt`, remove them after updating.

## Already running

**Classic install:** update with the command under **Updating** below.

**bootc install:** `sudo bootc switch ghcr.io/vivalavent/ps5-bazzite-bootc:v2.5`, then restart.

## New install

**Classic (recommended for most):** flash the full image from the [latest stable release](https://github.com/VivaLaVent/ps5-bazzite-deck/releases/latest), start it once, then update to v2.5 with the command under **Updating** above.

**bootc (experimental):** needs a drive of 64 GB or more.
1. Extract `ps5-bazzite-deck-bootc-v2.5.img.7z.001` with 7-Zip.
2. Flash the `.img`: on Windows with [Rufus](https://rufus.ie) in **DD mode** or [Balena Etcher](https://etcher.balena.io); on Linux or macOS with `sudo dd if=ps5-bazzite-deck-bootc-v2.5.img of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress` (replace `/dev/sdX` with your drive).
3. Start it with `bazzite-bootc-loader-v2.5.elf`.

**First start, either way:** Steam downloads and updates itself before Game Mode appears, which can take 10 to 15 minutes on a black screen. Leave it running.

## What to test

- **FLAVA3 consoles:** every resolution Game Mode offers (1080p, 1440p, 4K at 60 and 120 Hz), HDR, and VRR with Steam's VRR setting on.
- **Verde consoles:** your usual resolution, after sleep and after switching between Game Mode and the desktop.
- **Every console:** Wi-Fi and Bluetooth after a cold start.
