## What's new

### 4K over HDMI 2.1, and 1440p back

For FLAVA3 consoles with an HDMI 2.1 TV:

- **Game Mode at 4K60 and 4K120 over HDMI 2.1 (FRL).** v2.4.1 fixed half of the 4K black screen; a second check in the new 4K planning still refused Game Mode's picture whenever FRL was on. HDR at 4K60 uses the classic HDMI connection, which carries it fine.
- **1440p60 and 1440p120 are offered again** for TVs and monitors that list 1440p or larger modes, as in v2.1.x.

If you added `hdmi.frl_4k120=0` to `cmdline.txt`, remove it after updating.

### Known issues

- VRR is offered but doesn't engage yet on the new kernel base.
- HDR at 4K120 isn't available yet; 4K60 HDR works.

## Already running

**Classic install:** update with the command under **Updating** below.

**bootc install:** `sudo bootc switch ghcr.io/vivalavent/ps5-bazzite-bootc:v2.4.2`, then restart.

## New install

**Classic (recommended for most):** flash the full image from the [latest stable release](https://github.com/VivaLaVent/ps5-bazzite-deck/releases/latest), start it once, then update to v2.4.2 with the command under **Updating** above.

**bootc (experimental):** needs a drive of 64 GB or more.
1. Extract `ps5-bazzite-deck-bootc-v2.4.2.img.7z.001` with 7-Zip.
2. Flash the `.img`: on Windows with [Rufus](https://rufus.ie) in **DD mode** or [Balena Etcher](https://etcher.balena.io); on Linux or macOS with `sudo dd if=ps5-bazzite-deck-bootc-v2.4.2.img of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress` (replace `/dev/sdX` with your drive).
3. Start it with `bazzite-bootc-loader-v2.4.2.elf`.

**First start, either way:** Steam downloads and updates itself before Game Mode appears, which can take 10 to 15 minutes on a black screen. Leave it running.

## What to test

- **FLAVA3 consoles with an HDMI 2.1 TV:** Game Mode at 4K60, then 4K120 and 1440p120 in Settings, Display; HDR on at 4K60.
- **Every console:** your usual resolution, 1440p where your display offers it, switching between Game Mode and the desktop.
